# Content Research Notes — Etheys Fashion Feaster

## Organisation
Etheys Fashion Feaster is a fictional retail/small-business organisation created for the WEBD6211 academic project. The scenario is based in Limpopo, South Africa.

## Audience
- Young adults
- Parents and families
- Customers looking for affordable everyday fashion
- Customers needing product, size and availability information

## Page Content Plan
### Home
Hero statement, short introduction, value propositions and call-to-action buttons.

### About Us
Fictional history, mission, vision and organisational values.

### Products
Women’s, men’s and children’s collections with illustrative prices and enquiry buttons.

### Enquiry
Customer name, email, phone, enquiry type and message.

### Contact
Two proposed locations, contact information and map/location visual.

## Content Approach
All organisation-specific text is original academic scenario content. No claim is made that Etheys Fashion Feaster is a real operating business.
