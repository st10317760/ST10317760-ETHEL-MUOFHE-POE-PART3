ETHEYS FASHION FEASTER
STEP 3 AND 4 – CONTENT RESEARCH AND SOURCING

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
Etheys Fashion Feaster is a proposed fashion retail brand that will use a professional website to present clothing and fashion products to customers. The website will provide information about the business, display products, provide a visual gallery, and make it easy for customers to contact the business.

2. RESEARCH PURPOSE
The research was conducted to identify the content and website features needed for a modern fashion website. The research focused on:
- Website usability and clear navigation.
- Responsive design for phones, tablets, and computers.
- Product presentation.
- Accessible and readable web content.
- Appropriate use of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Organised website structure and content.
- Image sourcing and attribution.

3. TARGET AUDIENCE
The primary audience is fashion-conscious customers, particularly students, young adults, and working adults seeking attractive, affordable clothing. The website is designed for visitors who want to browse products quickly, read useful information, and contact the business.

4. CONTENT REQUIREMENTS
The website requires:
- Business name and branding.
- Home page introduction.
- About Us information.
- Mission and vision.
- Product names and descriptions.
- Product prices where applicable.
- Gallery content.
- Contact information.
- Calls-to-action.
- Navigation labels.
- Footer information.

5. WEBSITE CONTENT PLAN

HOME PAGE
Headline: Discover Your Style with Etheys Fashion Feaster.
Purpose: Introduce the brand and direct visitors to products.

ABOUT PAGE
Etheys Fashion Feaster is a fashion-focused business concept committed to presenting stylish, accessible, and customer-focused fashion. The website supports the business by giving customers a convenient place to learn about the brand and explore products.

Mission:
To provide customers with a convenient and enjoyable way to discover fashionable clothing through a professional digital platform.

Vision:
To build a trusted fashion brand with a strong digital presence and a customer-focused shopping experience.

PRODUCTS PAGE
Products will be presented in cards containing:
- Product image.
- Product name.
- Short description.
- Price where applicable.
- Product category.

Suggested product categories:
- Dresses.
- Tops.
- Bottoms.
- Sets.
- Accessories.

GALLERY PAGE
The gallery will display approved fashion photographs supplied by the student/business. Images should be relevant, clear and suitable for the website.

CONTACT PAGE
The contact page will include:
- Customer name.
- Email address.
- Message.
- Contact button.
- Business contact details.

7. ACCESSIBILITY AND USABILITY RESEARCH
The website will use meaningful page titles, semantic HTML, readable text, visible focus states, descriptive alternative text for images, and clear form labels. W3C identifies accessibility as an important part of web development, and WCAG provides guidance for accessible web content.

8. RESPONSIVE DESIGN RESEARCH
The website will use a responsive approach so that content adapts to different screen sizes. CSS media queries and flexible layouts will be used. The HTML pages will include the viewport meta tag so mobile browsers render the page appropriately.

9. TECHNOLOGY RESEARCH
HTML5 will provide the structure and semantic content.
CSS3 will control layout, typography, spacing, navigation, and responsive behavior.
JavaScript will provide appropriate interaction and client-side form validation.
